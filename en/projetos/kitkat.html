KitKat

A fresh take on the break: more spontaneous, playful and full of personality.

As part of an educational project in partnership with Nestlé, we were challenged to experiment with the KitKat Moments line, with the freedom to rethink its name, identity and packaging.

The brief called for a complete product system: a stand-up pouch, three finger flavors and a chocolate bar, all connected by a new visual identity.

We began by looking at the brand’s best-known concept: the break. Rather than simply repeating the traditional idea of a pause, we sought to give this moment a new meaning, turning it into a more contemporary, playful experience specific to the line.

This change led to a new name and brand personality, designed to connect with its audience in a more spontaneous, relevant way. At the same time, we preserved the essential elements that make KitKat recognizable, balancing familiarity and novelty.

The result is a reinterpretation of the brand that makes taking a break more expressive, inviting and visually striking: a new way to understand the famous break.
