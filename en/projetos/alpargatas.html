Hub Alpargatas

From Mooca’s railway tracks to paths of innovation, an identity shaped by encounters and movement.

This project began with a proposal to create the visual identity of an innovation hub for Alpargatas, drawing on its origins and legacy. The starting point was Mooca, a neighborhood shaped by railway tracks, movement and work.

The aesthetic was built around railway tracks as a metaphor for connection and transformation. Just as different routes can intersect and lead to new destinations, innovation emerges when ideas, people and possibilities meet.

The project represents this movement visually, creating an identity that can accompany different journeys and experiences within the hub. The tracks go beyond a physical route to symbolize discovery, learning and collective creation.

The identity is therefore more than a representation of the space: it is a system that values the process of innovation. Through routes, connections and new destinations, the project reinforces the idea that every journey helps build the future and leaves a legacy for those who follow.
