Fósforo Queluz

The energy of 1950s Brazilian posters turns an everyday object into packaging full of personality.

For this academic packaging project, we worked with Fósforo Queluz, seeking to recover its history while creating an identity that could stand out in a market lacking originality and presence.

The main inspiration came from Brazilian posters of the 1950s, exploring a vintage aesthetic shaped by the graphic communication of that period. From these references, we built a rich visual language using illustrations, drawings, halftones, screen patterns and graphic textures, alongside playful, expressive and dynamic typography. The aim was to create a composition full of movement and personality, recalling the popular, striking character of old posters.

Brazilian cultural identity was also central to the design. We sought references that offered a visual interpretation of Brazilian culture rather than simply reproducing a retro aesthetic. Colors, illustrations, shapes and textures were combined to create packaging with its own identity and a closer connection to its audience.

The result combines 1950s vintage style with a playful, dynamic and distinctly Brazilian graphic language. It transforms an everyday product into a visually striking piece, deliberately using a variety of graphic elements to give Fósforo Queluz personality and help it stand out at the point of sale.
